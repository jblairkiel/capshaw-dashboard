Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

The Member Portal at capshaw.jblairkiel.com is run by volunteers for the members and friends of Capshaw Church of Christ in Harvest, Alabama. This page explains what we keep about you when you use it, why, who can see it, and how to have it removed. We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use it for anything other than running the portal for our church family.

What we keep

When you sign in with Google or Facebook, they share with us your name, your email address, a link to your profile picture, and an account number that tells us it is you the next time. We ask only for your public profile and email address. We never see your Google or Facebook password, and we cannot post anything or read your friends, messages or other activity.

When you create an account with an email address, we keep your name, your email address, and your password in scrambled form (a one-way hash), never the password itself.

About your account, we also keep when it was created, when you last signed in, whether it has been approved and by whom, which entry in the church directory it belongs to, and what parts of the portal you may use.

What you add. Anything you type into the portal, such as profile details, sign-ups, comments, worship preferences, time away, or a problem report (which includes the page you were on and the name of your browser), is kept so the portal can show it to the people it is meant for.

A record of changes. When someone changes information in the portal, we note who made the change and when, so mistakes can be traced and put right.

Cookies

We use one cookie, to keep you signed in. It lasts seven days, or until you sign out. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not use Facebook or Google to follow you around other websites.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account safe.
  • To let the church office approve your account and connect it to your entry in the member directory.
  • To show you, and the people you serve alongside, the schedules, sign-ups, groups and announcements the portal exists for.
  • To email you about your account (such as a confirmation link, an approval notice, or a password reset) and about church business you have been signed up for.

Who can see it

Only signed-in, approved members of the portal, and only what each page is meant to show. Your email address and account details are visible to the portal's administrators, who are members of the congregation helping run it.

Approved members can add the member directory — names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses and family photos — to their own phone's contacts, so they can reach one another. Each time somebody does, it is recorded. Our terms ask them to keep those details to themselves.

We do not sell, rent or give your information to anyone. A few services handle it on our behalf only so the portal can work: the computer that hosts the portal, the email service that delivers its messages, and Google or Facebook when you choose to sign in with them. The portal's Bible class tools send lesson topics to an AI service to help write questions; they do not send anything about you.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. If you ask us to delete it, we remove it as described below. Links we email you stop working after an hour (password resets) or two days (email confirmations).

Deleting your account and information

You can ask us to delete your account at any time. Call the church office at (256) 771-0390, speak to one of the portal administrators at church, or call or text one of the administrators listed on the portal's waiting page. Tell us the name and email address on the account and, if you used it, whether you signed in with Google or Facebook.

When we delete your account we remove your sign-in details: your name, email address, profile picture link, password hash and Google or Facebook account number, along with your notifications and the account's access. Things you added that belong to the congregation, such as a sign-up or a comment, may stay with your name on them; ask and we will remove those too. The record of changes keeps a note that the account was removed. Your entry in the church's own member directory is separate from the portal account; the church office can update or remove that as well.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also stop the portal receiving anything further from Facebook: on Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, find the Capshaw portal and choose Remove. Then contact us as above to have what we already hold deleted.

If you signed in with Google, you can do the same from your Google Account under Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Children

The portal is meant for the adults of our congregation. Information about children in the congregation is entered and kept by the church office as part of the member directory, not collected from children through the portal.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we keep or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.
